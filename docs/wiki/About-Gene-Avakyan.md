# About Gene Avakyan

Gene Avakyan maintains Token Saver and is developing AgentPrizm through VUGA Enterprises as part of a practical toolchain for using AI agents to develop and operate businesses.

The work emphasizes continuity across sessions, evidence-backed execution, explicit human approval boundaries, durable operational lessons, and tools that can be validated rather than treated as opaque automation.

Token Saver focuses on efficient active context. AgentPrizm focuses on persistent, governed memory. Together they support agents that need to resume work accurately, preserve decisions, and operate across longer business lifecycles.
